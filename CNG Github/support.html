Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a CNG slot?
To book a slot: Login to your account, search for nearby pumps using our Locator map, select your preferred pump, pick an available 15-minute slot date & time on the booking panel, complete mock payment, and retrieve your ticket QR code.
Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?
Yes. You can manage, review, and cancel active slots anytime from the "Dashboard / History" page. Clicking the cancel button frees up the pump queue instantly and initiates deposit refund simulation.
What payment methods are supported?
We support simulated UPI QR code scans and debit/credit card entries. A small refuel advance deposit of ₹100 is processed mock-safely to secure the slot timing.
What happens if I arrive late for my slot?
We hold priority slots for up to 10 minutes past the scheduled time. If you exceed this grace period, your booking will remain active but you may experience slightly longer queues at the station.
Are queue waiting times real?
Yes, our system estimates current queues by tracking active bookings at each station and simulating live arrival fluctuations dynamically every few seconds.
