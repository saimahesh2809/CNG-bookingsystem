About CNG Booking System

Our Story

The CNG Booking System was developed by Sai Developers as part of a school FA project. The inspiration came from seeing long queues of taxis and private autos waiting for CNG late at night on Hyderabad roads. Our mission is simple: help drivers save valuable time and fuel by allowing them to check queues and book priority slots in advance.

Our Mission

We aim to modernize the CNG refueling experience through technology. By reducing waiting times and optimizing refueling schedules, this system not only makes life easier for commercial and private drivers but also contributes to a more efficient, orderly, and eco-friendly clean energy transport ecosystem.

Project Highlights

  • Queue Optimization: Real-time estimation of wait lines based on slot distribution.
  • Priority Refueling: Check-in QR pass system allowing rapid validation.
  • Reduced Emissions: Decreasing vehicle idling times while waiting in queues.
  • Convenience & Security: Mock UPI/Card advance checkout options.
